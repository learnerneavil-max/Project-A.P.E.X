# Cybernetics (The Science of Steering)

Cybernetics is the study of **Feedback Loops** in systems. 

You cannot control a complex system (like your life, a business, or your body) by giving it a rigid set of rules or relying on willpower. You can only control it by managing its feedback mechanisms.

### The Developer View
A thermostat is a cybernetic system. It doesn't use willpower to stay warm; it just knows "if it drops below 68 degrees, turn on the furnace." 

Apply this to your life: build a cybernetic environment where failure automatically triggers a corrective action without requiring conscious motivation.
