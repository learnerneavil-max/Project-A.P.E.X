# Network Architecture & Trust Engineering

You operate in a complex human network. 

### Core Operating Rules:
* **Strength of Weak Ties:** The most valuable opportunities come from the outer edges of your network, not your close friends.
* **The Spotlight Effect:** Nobody is paying as much attention to you as you think. Leverage this to take social risks without fear.
* **Signal vs. Noise:** Be unconditionally polite and punctual. This instantly builds a high-signal reputation for competence.
