# Asymmetric Career Capital

Linear career paths are a trap. You don't win by being the absolute best at one thing.

### Core Operating Rules:
* **Skill Stacking:** Combine multiple average skills (e.g., coding + copywriting + basic design) to create a highly unique, irreplaceable profile.
* **The R&D Phase:** Your 20s are a research phase, not a deadline. Gather maximum data.
* **Side Quests:** Opportunities rarely come from doing exactly what is asked. They come from the low-risk, high-reward asymmetric bets you take on the side.
